   ### B. Problem Statement
Students of Babcock University experience challenges when searching for scholarships, internships, competitions, technology programmes and other career-related opportunities. Based on the responses collected through our Babcock University Student Opportunity Discovery Survey, these challenges include difficulty accessing reliable information, late discovery of opportunities, limited exposure and the lack of a centralised system for finding and managing opportunities.

One of the major findings from our survey was that 86.4% of the 22 respondents reported having missed an opportunity because they discovered it too late. This highlights the difficulty students experience in staying informed about available opportunities and their application deadlines.

The survey also revealed that students discover opportunities through different sources, including websites, friends and classmates, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and university announcements.

 Websites and friends or classmates each accounted for 22.7% of responses. This shows that opportunity information is spread across multiple channels rather than being available in one organised location.

Another challenge is the difficulty of finding opportunities that are relevant to individual students. When asked how frequently they come across opportunities that are relevant to them, 45.5% answered sometimes, while 31.8% answered rarely. This suggests that students do not consistently encounter opportunities that match their interests, qualifications and academic backgrounds.

Managing application deadlines is also a concern. According to the survey, 27.3% of respondents indicated that they do not keep track of application deadlines, while others rely on reminders, calendars and notes applications. This demonstrates the need for a more organised way of monitoring opportunity deadlines.

In addition, responses to the open-ended survey questions highlighted concerns about fraudulent opportunities, scams, incorrect information, limited exposure and difficulties identifying genuine opportunities and understanding their eligibility requirements.

The survey also showed positive interest in a centralised solution, with all 20 respondents who answered the question about using a platform that brings different student opportunities together indicating that they would use such a platform.

These findings establish the need to investigate a centralised opportunity discovery platform that can make relevant opportunities easier to access, improve information circulation, provide clearer application deadlines and help students manage their search for educational and career opportunities.

  ### C. Project Purpose
The purpose of the Babcock Opportunity & Career Hub is to provide a centralised digital platform where Babcock University students, final-year students and graduates can discover, access and manage educational and career-related opportunities.

The platform is intended to reduce the difficulty of searching through multiple information sources by bringing opportunities such as scholarships, internships, competitions, technology programmes, graduate trainee programmes and job openings into one accessible location.

It also aims to improve the way students interact with these opportunities by providing features that support opportunity discovery, application processes, deadline awareness and application tracking.

Furthermore, the platform seeks to create a connection between students, graduates and organisations, allowing organisations to share opportunities while giving students easier access to potential educational and career development pathways.

Ultimately, the project aims to make opportunity discovery more organised, accessible and convenient for the Babcock University community.

  ### D. Project Objectives
The Babcock Opportunity & Career Hub aims to provide a centralised platform where students and graduates can easily discover and access scholarships, internships, competitions, technology programmes and employment opportunities. The platform seeks to simplify opportunity discovery by bringing relevant listings together in one place, allowing users to create professional profiles, upload CVs, apply for opportunities and track their applications. It also aims to connect students and graduates with organisations, provide a reliable system for reviewing and approving opportunity submissions, and improve deadline awareness through application deadline tracking and automatic expiration of expired opportunities. Ultimately, the project seeks to make accessing educational and career opportunities more organised, convenient and accessible to the Babcock University community.

   ### E. Target Users
The Babcock Opportunity & Career Hub is designed for three main categories of users: students and graduates, companies and organisations, and platform administrators. Students and graduates will use the platform to create accounts, build professional profiles, upload CVs, discover relevant opportunities, submit applications, save listings and track their application progress. Companies and organisations will use the platform to create organisation profiles, publish scholarships, internships, job openings and other opportunities, as well as review applications and applicants' CVs. Administrators will oversee the platform by managing user accounts, reviewing and approving submitted opportunities, monitoring published listings and handling reports to ensure that the platform operates effectively and that available information remains reliable.

   ### F. Product Scope
The initial version of the Babcock Opportunity & Career Hub will focus on providing the essential features needed to connect students and graduates with organisations offering educational and career-related opportunities. The platform will include user registration and login, student and graduate profiles, CV uploads, organisation accounts, opportunity submission and posting, administrator review and approval, opportunity discovery and filtering, saving opportunities, job and internship applications, and application tracking. It will also include application deadline management, allowing users to view closing dates while automatically marking opportunities as expired once their deadlines have passed. The platform will support applications through its internal application system or external application links, depending on the organisation's submission process. Features beyond these essential functions may be considered for future versions as the platform develops.
 ## 2.Research and Validation
 ### A. Research Methodology
    The research for the Babcock Opportunity & Career Hub was conducted to understand the challenges students face when discovering and accessing scholarships, internships, competitions, technology programmes and other career-related opportunities. A Google Forms questionnaire titled "Babcock University Student Opportunity Discovery Survey" was used to collect responses from students. The survey gathered information about how students currently discover opportunities, how frequently they come across relevant opportunities, whether they have missed opportunities due to late discovery, the challenges they experience when searching for opportunities and how they manage application deadlines. It also explored students' experiences with existing opportunity platforms and their interest in using a centralised platform that brings different opportunities together. A total of 22 responses were collected and analysed to identify common challenges, understand students' needs and evaluate the relevance of the proposed solution.
  
   ### B. Survey Findings
The Babcock University Student Opportunity Discovery Survey received a total of 22 responses. The findings provided insight into how students discover opportunities, how frequently they encounter relevant opportunities and the challenges they experience during their search.

When asked where they usually discover opportunities such as scholarships, internships and competitions, respondents identified several sources, including websites (22.7%), friends and classmates (22.7%), LinkedIn (18.2%), WhatsApp (13.6%), university announcements (9.1%), Instagram (9.1%) and Snapchat. These findings show that opportunity information reaches students through different channels.

Regarding how frequently respondents come across opportunities that are relevant to them, 45.5% selected sometimes, 31.8% selected rarely, 13.6% selected very often and 9.1% selected often. This suggests that many respondents do not consistently come across opportunities that match their interests and qualifications.

One significant finding was that 86.4% of respondents reported having missed an opportunity because they discovered it too late, while 9.1% said no and the remaining respondents were unsure. This highlights the importance of timely access to opportunity information.

The survey also explored whether respondents had used platforms specifically designed for finding opportunities. Of the 22 respondents, 36.4% answered yes, 31.8% answered no and 31.8% were unsure. This indicates varying levels of experience with dedicated opportunity platforms.

In relation to application deadline management, 40.9% of respondents reported using reminders, 27.3% indicated that they do not keep track of deadlines, while others mentioned using calendars and notes applications. This suggests that students use different methods to manage application deadlines.

Furthermore, 16 respondents provided answers about the challenges they experience when searching for opportunities. Their responses included difficulty finding relevant opportunities, limited opportunities, lack of correct information, difficulty identifying genuine opportunities, concerns about fraud and scams, lengthy application processes and limited exposure to available opportunities.

When asked whether they would use a platform that brings different student opportunities together in one place, all 20 respondents who answered the question selected yes, representing 100% of responses to that question.

Overall, the survey findings reveal challenges surrounding opportunity discovery, information accessibility, late awareness of opportunities and deadline management. They also indicate positive interest in having a centralised platform where students can discover and access different educational and career-related opportunities.

  ### C. Key Research Insights
The research revealed that students experience difficulties accessing relevant and reliable opportunities due to information being distributed across multiple platforms and communication channels. A significant number of respondents have missed opportunities because they discovered them too late, while many do not consistently come across opportunities that match their interests and qualifications. The findings also highlight challenges with identifying genuine opportunities, accessing correct information and keeping track of application deadlines. Different students currently depend on websites, social media, friends, classmates and university announcements to discover opportunities, showing the absence of a single organised source. Furthermore, all 22 respondents who answered the question about using a centralised opportunity platform expressed willingness to use one. These insights support the need for a platform that brings different opportunities together, improves information accessibility, provides clear deadlines and helps students organise their opportunity searches.

 ### D. Existing Solutions and Alternatives
Students currently rely on different platforms and communication channels to discover scholarships, internships, competitions and employment opportunities. These include WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram, university announcements, websites, friends and classmates. Dedicated employment and opportunity platforms such as Jobberman and Indeed also provide access to job listings and career opportunities. However, relying on multiple sources can make it difficult for students to find relevant information, monitor application deadlines and keep track of available opportunities. Some students may also encounter outdated information, misleading announcements or opportunities that do not match their qualifications. The Babcock Opportunity & Career Hub is proposed as a more centralised solution that brings different categories of opportunities together while providing organised listings, eligibility information, application deadlines and access to application processes. Rather than replacing every existing platform, it aims to make opportunity discovery easier by providing students with one organised place to access relevant information.

## 6. Proposed Solution and User Workflow
The proposed solution is a web-based opportunity discovery and career platform that connects students and graduates with organisations offering educational and professional opportunities.

The process begins when users register and create accounts based on their user categories. Students and graduates can build their profiles, upload CVs and explore available opportunities, while organisations can create profiles and submit opportunity listings. Submitted opportunities will be sent to administrators for review and approval. Once approved, the opportunities will become visible to students and graduates, who can view their details, check eligibility requirements and application deadlines, save listings and submit applications through the platform or external application links. Opportunities will automatically be marked as expired after their application deadlines have passed.
## 7. Functional Requirements
The platform should support:

Registration and Authentication: Account creation, login and user permissions.

User Profiles: Profile creation, updates and CV uploads.

Organisation Management: Organisation registration and opportunity submission.

Opportunity Management: Publishing, searching, filtering, saving and automatic expiration of listings.

Administrator Management: Reviewing submissions, managing users and handling reports.

Application Management: Internal and external applications, including application tracking where supported.

## 8. Non-Fuctional Requirements
The platform should be secure, reliable, user-friendly and accessible on mobile phones, tablets and computers. It should protect user information and uploaded CVs, provide reasonable loading speeds and support future growth and improvements.
## 9. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) of the Babcock Opportunity & Career Hub will be a functional web-based platform that allows Babcock University students and graduates to discover educational and career-related opportunities from one central location.

The first version will focus on the essential features required to make opportunity discovery easier and more organised.

The MVP will include the following features:

User Registration and Login: Students and graduates will be able to create accounts and securely log in to the platform.

User Profiles: Users will be able to create and update basic profiles containing relevant personal and academic information.

Opportunity Submission: Verified organisations and authorised administrators will be able to submit opportunities such as scholarships, internships, competitions and job openings.

Opportunity Approval: Administrators will review submitted opportunities before approving them for publication.

Opportunity Listings: Users will be able to view approved opportunities with important details such as title, organisation, description, eligibility requirements, application deadline and application link.

Search and Filtering: Users will be able to search for opportunities and filter them by category.

Application Access: Users will be able to access application links and apply for opportunities through the platform or the organisation's external application page.

Deadline Management: The platform will display application deadlines and automatically mark expired opportunities as closed.

The MVP will prioritise these essential features before introducing additional functionalities such as email notifications, advanced application tracking, organisation dashboards and analytics in future versions.

## 11. Acceptance Criteria
The platform should allow users to register and access their appropriate features. Students should be able to explore opportunities and apply, organisations should be able to submit listings, and administrators should be able to approve or reject submissions. Approved listings should appear publicly, while expired opportunities should be removed from active listings. Users should also be able to access their application records where internal applications are supported.
